ABOUT — PRANAMYYA SHAKYA

I build enterprise AI that people actually adopt.

I'm a Product & AI Transformation Leader working at the intersection of voice, search and autonomous agents. My focus is narrow and practical: take AI from impressive demo to production system that real teams use every day — in finance, logistics, healthcare and enterprise operations.

Across the ventures I lead, the pattern is consistent. Voice agents that handle live inbound at sub-400ms latency. Enterprise search that answers from contracts, SOPs and tickets with citations. Agent networks that route work across calling, collections and operations. And the workflow automation that ties it together. The through-line isn't the model — it's adoption.
