Enterprise search with RAG: citations beat confidence

A practical take on retrieval-augmented generation for internal knowledge.

A fluent answer with no source is a liability in an enterprise. The legal team will not act on "the renewal terms are 60 days" unless they can click through to the clause. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) earns trust by showing its work.

Retrieve, then generate

RAG finds the relevant passages across your contracts, SOPs and tickets, then generates an answer grounded in those passages — with a link back to each source. The model is constrained to what your documents actually say, which is what makes it safe for finance and legal.

Access control is part of the product

Enterprise search is scoped to private knowledge and respects per-document permissions. A support agent sees what they are allowed to see; an auditor sees the trail. That boundary is not a feature add-on — it is the reason the system is allowed to exist.

The metric that matters

We do not optimise for "the answer looks right." We optimise for "the user clicked the citation and confirmed it." Verification, not confidence, is the adoption signal.
