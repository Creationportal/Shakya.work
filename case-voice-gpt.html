Voice GPT

Enterprise voice agent platform — from concept to 40+ production deployments

ROLE: PRODUCT LEAD · SCOPE: 0→1 → SCALE · STACK: ASR + LLM + TTS + CRM · STATUS: IN PRODUCTION

PRODUCT VISUAL — VOICE GPT LIVE CONSOLE · 16:9

OVERVIEW

Enterprise service lines were drowning in repetitive inbound calls — long queues, burned-out agents, inconsistent answers. The client needed coverage that scaled without headcount growth.

OUTCOME

Voice agents now resolve the majority of inbound calls end-to-end — intent detection, escalation to humans when confidence drops, and CRM write-back on every conversation.

03 — APPROACH

How we shipped it

01
Discover

Mapped the inbound call mix, found that 60% of volume was repetitive — and defined the agent's scope against real tickets, not assumptions.

02
Pilot

Ran a 3-week shadow pilot on one service line — agents assisted, humans stayed in the loop, and we tuned escalation thresholds with live data.

03
Scale

Rolled out across lines with observability and a CRM write-back contract — then handed the team a playbook to onboard the next vertical in days, not months.
