DISCIPLINE 02 — SEARCH

Enterprise search that cites its sources.

Retrieval-augmented generation over your private knowledge — contracts, SOPs, tickets and wikis. Ask a question in plain language and get an answer with citations back to the exact clause or revision. Built for finance, legal and support teams who cannot afford a hallucination.

CAPABILITIES

What enterprise search delivers

SEMANTIC

Meaning-based retrieval, not keyword matching — finds the right passage even with different wording.

CITED

Every answer links to the source document and passage for verification.

SECURE

Per-document access control; users only search what they are allowed to see.

CONNECTORS

Ingest from document stores, wikis and ticketing systems on a schedule.

USE CASES

Where it pays off

  • Contract lookup — "What are the Q2 renewal terms in Contract #1183?" returns the clause with a link.
  • SOP discovery — surface the latest approved procedure without digging through folders.
  • Ticket resolution — agents get grounded answers from past tickets in seconds.
  • Compliance — auditors trace every answer to its source of record.

FAQ

Common questions

What is enterprise search with RAG?

Retrieval-augmented generation finds relevant passages across internal documents and generates an answer grounded in those sources, with citations back to the original file.

How is enterprise search different from Google?

It is scoped to your private knowledge, respects per-document access control, and returns cited answers instead of a list of links.

Does enterprise search cite its sources?

Yes. Every answer links back to the specific contract clause, SOP revision or ticket it was drawn from.
