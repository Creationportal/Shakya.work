Privacy Policy

Plain-language details on what data this site collects, why, who it is shared with, and your rights.

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Overview

This site (shakya.work) is a personal portfolio and product site operated by Pranamyya Shakya ("shakya", "we", "us"). It is a static website — there is no account system, no login, and no advertising or third-party ad tracking. We collect only the minimum data needed to respond to you and to render the site.

What personal data we collect

  • Name and email address — submitted voluntarily through the contact form, along with the message you type. These are the only pieces of personal data you are asked to provide.
  • IP address and request metadata — when the page loads, your browser fetches web fonts from Google Fonts. To do that, Google receives your IP address, browser and operating-system details, and the referring page URL, exactly as it would for any site using Google Fonts.
  • Technical logs — our web host may retain standard server access logs (IP address, requested path, user agent, timestamp) for security and uptime. These are not used to identify you or build a profile.

We do not use analytics cookies, advertising identifiers, cross-site trackers, or fingerprinting. We do not sell or rent any personal data.

Why we collect it

  • The contact-form name and email let us reply to your message.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typography that makes the site readable and on-brand.
  • Server logs help us keep the site running and defend against abuse.

Who we share it with

  • Google LLC — receives your IP address and request metadata solely to serve the Google Fonts files. Google's handling of this data is governed by its Privacy Policy.
  • Our contact-form processor — form submissions are transmitted to a third-party form service (Formspree) so we can receive and reply to them. That provider processes the data on our behalf under its own privacy terms.

We do not share your data with anyone else except where required by law.

How we protect it

  • The site is served over HTTPS, so form submissions and font requests are encrypted in transit.
  • Access to submitted messages is limited to the site owner.
  • We keep contact-form messages only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry, then delete them.

Your rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your personal data. We honour equivalent rights for everyone:

  • Access — ask what data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion — ask us to erase your data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Opt-out / do-not-sell — we do not sell data, but you can object to processing and ask us to stop using your data for any purpose.
  • Portability — request a copy of the data you provided, in a common format.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at creationpanel@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by law (generally within 30 days). You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Data-request contact
