HOW IT WORKS

From ring to resolution

  1. Call connects — telephony hands audio to the agent.
  2. ASR transcribes speech; the LLM detects intent in real time.
  3. The agent answers, retrieves data, or routes to the right queue.
  4. TTS speaks the response; the loop continues naturally.
  5. Call ends — transcript and outcome are logged for QA.

FAQ

Common questions

What is an enterprise voice agent?

A phone system that uses ASR to transcribe speech, an LLM to understand intent and decide a response, and TTS to speak back — handling real calls without a human on the line.

How fast are enterprise voice agents?

Production systems run end-to-end latency around 380ms — fast enough for natural turn-taking on a live call.

Do enterprise voice agents support Chinese?

Yes. Multilingual voice agents support English and Chinese (中文) with language detection and code-switching within a single call.
