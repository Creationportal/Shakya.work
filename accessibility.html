Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making shakya.work usable by as many people as possible.

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Conformance target

WCAG 2.1 AA WCAG 2.2 AA

shakya.work aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 and 2.2 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, and improve usability for everyone.

Measures we take

  • Semantic HTML with a logical heading order and a "skip to content" link.
  • Visible keyboard focus styles and full operation by keyboard.
  • Responsive layout that works from mobile to desktop, and support for prefers-reduced-motion.
  • A light/dark theme toggle for reader comfort.
  • Page colours chosen for sufficient text contrast (see known gaps below).

Known gaps (honest status)

We are not yet fully compliant and are actively improving. Known limitations include:

  • Contrast: low-contrast text on some surfaces was identified in a recent review and has since been fixed in the current build, but older cached copies may still show the weaker contrast.
  • JavaScript required: the site is a single-page application — its main content and interactive demos (including the trading simulator) require JavaScript to render, so they are not available without it.
  • Interactive widgets: canvas-based visualisations (animations, charts, the trading simulator) are decorative or illustrative and may not expose a fully equivalent non-visual alternative.
  • Third-party content: web fonts loaded from Google Fonts are outside our direct control.

We review the site against WCAG as part of ongoing maintenance and prioritise fixes that affect core reading and navigation.

Compatibility

The Site is designed to work with current versions of major browsers and common assistive technologies (screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, zoom and text-spacing). If you encounter a specific incompatibility, please tell us using the channel below.

Feedback & contact

If you find an accessibility barrier or have a suggestion, we want to hear from you:

creationpanel@gmail.com

We aim to read every message and to respond within a reasonable time. This statement will be updated as improvements ship.

This statement applies to shakya.work. It does not cover third-party sites we may link to.
